Privacy, in plain language.

Voxink processes the information needed to provide voice input, translation, meetings, documents, accounts, subscriptions and support. We do not sell personal data or use it for cross-company advertising tracking.

Tracking: No. Voxink does not link data with third-party data for targeted advertising or share it with data brokers. Product analytics tied to a Voxink identifier are used to run and improve Voxink.

Information we process

Account and identity

When you sign in, we may store your name, email address, sign-in provider identifier, Voxink user ID, account links, plan, quota and entitlement state. Depending on the platform and sign-in method, an account may also include a phone number or an approximate country used for account operations, security or analytics.

Voice and requested text processing

When you start recording, audio is sent to a speech provider for transcription. Direct streaming is normally transient. If a live connection fails, Voxink may temporarily buffer a bounded recovery recording on the device and server. Recovery files are removed after processing or failure, with automated cleanup for crash residue.

Transcribed, translated, polished, clipboard and actively selected text is sent to perform the feature you request. Ordinary voice typing is not kept as a dictation-history product. If "Help improve translations" is on, a copy of translation and polish text is kept as described below. Operational and security logs may contain content-free request metadata and controlled error categories.

Help improve translations

The Voxink app has a "Help improve translations" setting. It is on by default, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings. While it is on and you are signed in, Voxink keeps the text of your translate, clipboard-translate and polish requests (the text you sent and the result), with the language pair, mode, app version and platform. No audio is saved for this purpose. The copy is stored under a pseudonymous identifier instead of your account ID, deleted automatically after 90 days, and deleted with your account. It is used only to evaluate and improve Voxink's translation quality. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for their own use. Turning the setting off stops new text from being saved; text already saved is deleted within 90 days, or sooner on request to privacy@voxink.ai.

Meetings

Meeting transcripts and related metadata are saved to your account in the cloud so that you can review them later. You can delete a meeting at any time; meetings remain until you delete them or delete the account.

Documents and images

Document translation processes the source file or image, extracted text/OCR, translated content, previews and exports. For jobs created after the production retention cutoff, job content is scheduled for deletion within 24 hours. Some jobs created before enforcement began are isolated as legacy data and may remain longer while controlled migration and cleanup are completed. They are not exempt from deletion requests; contact us if you want associated legacy document data removed.

Content-free document telemetry—such as job status, format, language pair, page count, duration, tier and controlled error class—may be retained for up to 180 days. It does not intentionally contain document text, images, filenames or access tokens.

Analytics and diagnostics

We process product interactions, onboarding/funnel events, feature usage, app/platform version, plan and quota usage, error classes, and limited device or network identifiers for functionality, analytics, reliability, fraud prevention and support. Funnel events may be retained for up to 90 days. We do not intentionally place voice, transcript or document content in analytics events.

Payments and subscriptions

Apple, Google Play or Stripe handles payment credentials. Voxink receives transaction, product, subscription, entitlement and limited payment metadata needed to activate plans, reconcile purchases, prevent fraud and provide support. Voxink does not receive full card or bank-account details.

Feedback and support

If you contact support or submit feedback, we process the contact details, message, diagnostic context and any image or video you choose to attach. Do not include information that is not needed to resolve the issue.

Accessibility, keyboard and clipboard

Android Accessibility is optional. When enabled, Voxink may inspect the focused input state, current app context needed for the floating control, and text you actively select for translation. Selected text is held briefly on-device and sent only when you request translation. Voxink is designed not to read passwords, PINs, payment fields or unrelated screen content.

The iOS keyboard uses Full Access for network communication required by requested transcription and translation. Voxink does not use it to build a general keystroke history. Where automatic insertion is unavailable, Voxink may place a result on the system clipboard; clipboard behavior is also controlled by the operating system and other apps you authorize.

Retention, security and deletion

  • Temporary voice-recovery files are removed after processing/failure, with safety cleanup for abandoned files.
  • New document-job content is scheduled for deletion within 24 hours; the legacy exception is described above.
  • Document telemetry may remain up to 180 days and funnel events up to 90 days.
  • Text saved under "Help improve translations" is deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Account, meeting, quota and entitlement records remain while needed to provide the account or until deletion.
  • Transaction, chargeback, fraud, security, deleted-account tombstone and legally required records may remain for the applicable operational or legal period. Where practical, retained records are dissociated from the deleted account.

Data is encrypted in transit and production access is restricted. No security measure is perfect, but we use access controls and minimization appropriate to the service.

Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local data; it does not delete your server account. Use the account-deletion instructions or email privacy@voxink.ai.

Your choices and contact

You can turn off "Help improve translations" in the app's Settings, and disable microphone, Accessibility, overlay, keyboard Full Access and other optional permissions in system settings. You may request access, correction or deletion where applicable. Individual meeting or document controls may also be available in the product.

Privacy and deletion: privacy@voxink.ai

我们处理的信息

账号与身份

登录时，我们可能保存姓名、邮箱、登录提供方标识、Voxink 用户 ID、账号关联、套餐、额度和权益状态。根据平台与登录方式，账号还可能包含电话号码或用于账号运营、安全和分析的粗略国家/地区。

语音与用户主动提交的文字

开始录音后，音频会发送给语音服务商进行转写。直接流式音频通常是临时处理。实时连接失败时，Voxink 可能在设备和服务器暂存有大小与时长限制的恢复录音；处理完成、失败或发生崩溃残留时会清理。

转写、翻译、润色、剪贴板文字和用户主动选择的文字，在用户调用相应功能时发送处理。普通语音输入不会作为“听写历史”产品长期保存。“帮助改进翻译”开启时，翻译和润色文字会按下文说明保存一份。运营与安全日志可能保存不含正文的请求元数据和受控错误类别。

帮助改进翻译

Voxink App 设置里有“帮助改进翻译”开关，默认开启，可随时在设置中关闭。开关打开且已登录时，Voxink 保存翻译、剪贴板翻译和润色请求的文字（你发送的原文和结果），以及语言对、模式、App 版本和平台。这部分不包含任何录音。这些文字使用伪名标识保存，不使用你的账号 ID；90 天后自动删除，删除账号时一并删除。只用于评估和改进 Voxink 的翻译质量；不出售，不用于广告，也不提供给第三方自行使用。关闭开关后不再保存新的文字；已保存的在 90 天内删除，也可发邮件到 privacy@voxink.ai 要求提前删除。

会议

会议转写和相关元数据保存在你账号的云端，方便之后回看。你可以随时删除单场会议；会议记录在你删除或删除账号前一直保留。

文档与图片

文档翻译会处理源文件/图片、提取文字与 OCR、译文、预览和导出文件。生产 retention cutoff 之后创建的新任务，其内容计划在 24 小时内删除。强制规则启用前创建的少量 legacy 任务在受控迁移与清理完成前可能保留更久。用户仍可要求删除相关 legacy 文档数据。

不含正文的文档遥测（状态、格式、语言对、页数、耗时、档位与受控错误类别）最长可保留 180 天；不应包含文档正文、图片、文件名或访问 token。

分析与诊断

我们会处理产品交互、引导漏斗、功能使用、App/平台版本、套餐额度、错误类别及有限设备或网络标识，用于功能、分析、可靠性、防欺诈与支持。漏斗事件最长可保留 90 天；分析事件不应包含语音、转写正文或文档内容。

支付、反馈与支持

Apple、Google Play 或 Stripe 处理支付凭据；Voxink 接收交易、商品、订阅、权益及有限支付元数据，不接收完整银行卡或银行账户资料。联系支持或提交反馈时，我们会处理联系方式、消息、诊断上下文和用户主动附加的图片/视频。

辅助功能、键盘与剪贴板

Android 辅助功能是可选的。启用后，Voxink 可读取当前输入焦点、悬浮工具所需的 App 上下文，以及用户主动选择并要求翻译的文字。选择文字仅在设备短暂保留并在用户请求后发送。产品设计不读取密码、PIN、支付字段或无关屏幕内容。

iOS 键盘的“完全访问”用于用户请求的联网转写和翻译，不用于建立通用按键历史。无法自动写入时，结果可能放入系统剪贴板；剪贴板也受操作系统和用户授权的其他 App 控制。

服务商

Voxink 使用 Soniox（语音识别）、Google 服务（登录、AI 处理和云服务）、Apple（登录、App Store 与购买）、Google Play、Stripe（网页订阅结账）以及云端/API/文档处理服务商。服务商依据各自条款与隐私政策处理完成服务所需的信息。除非合同或公开条款已经确认，我们不会作出服务商“绝不训练”或特定保留期的绝对承诺。
